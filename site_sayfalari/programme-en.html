PURPOSE

The E-Commerce and Marketing Programme aims to train associate degree graduates who are equipped with basic theoretical and applied knowledge in e-commerce, digital marketing and brand communication as required by the digital economy; who have a developed awareness of professional ethics and legal responsibility; and who have communication, teamwork and entrepreneurship competences. By combining theoretical knowledge with sectoral practice, the programme prepares its graduates for working life as professionals who adapt to the changing conditions of the digital economy, think analytically and critically, and are committed to ethical values.

MISSION

The E-Commerce and Marketing Programme aims to train associate degree graduates equipped with basic theoretical and applied knowledge in e-commerce, digital marketing and brand communication as required by the digital economy, with a developed awareness of professional ethics and legal responsibility, and with communication, teamwork and entrepreneurship competences; and to provide applied education in cooperation with sector stakeholders.

VISION

To be a reputable associate degree programme that continuously improves its quality and, in cooperation with the sector, trains qualified graduates through practice-oriented education in line with accreditation criteria and current sectoral expectations.

PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES

  • PEA-1 — Employment and Professional Positioning: Our graduates take on professional responsibilities in e-commerce, digital marketing, retail and foreign trade, or in their own ventures.
  • PEA-2 — Professional Competence and Practice: Our graduates effectively use current tools, software and processes in e-commerce operations, digital marketing and brand communication.
  • PEA-3 — Ethical and Responsible Professional Conduct: Our graduates practise their profession in accordance with professional ethics, consumer rights, personal data protection and information technology legislation.
  • PEA-4 — Lifelong Learning and Career Development: Our graduates sustain their professional development by following sectoral and technological change, and specialise through bachelor's completion or certification programmes.

DEGREE AWARDED

Students who successfully complete the 124 ECTS programme and meet the programme qualifications are awarded the Associate Degree in E-Commerce and Marketing.

LEVEL OF DEGREE

Associate Degree (Short Cycle, TYYÇ Level 5)

ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Candidates must hold a high school diploma or equivalent and be placed through the central examination conducted by ÖSYM in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Council of Higher Education (YÖK).

RECOGNITION OF PRIOR LEARNING

The Regulation on Transfer, Double Major, Minor and Inter-Institutional Credit Transfer in Associate and Bachelor's Programmes, the Gaziantep University Associate and Bachelor's Education Regulation and the Gaziantep University Directive on the Recognition of Prior Learning apply. Applications are made within the calendar announced by the Directorate of Student Affairs.

QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS AND REGULATIONS

To obtain the associate degree in E-Commerce and Marketing, students must successfully complete all compulsory and elective courses of the programme and have a cumulative grade point average of at least 2.00 out of 4.00.

PROGRAMME PROFILE

The E-Commerce and Marketing Programme is a four-semester associate degree programme that provides students with a comprehensive grounding in the basic principles of digital commerce and marketing. The programme offers a broad range of knowledge and skills in e-commerce operations, digital marketing strategies, brand communication, data analysis, social media management, customer relationship management, e-commerce law and security, artificial intelligence literacy, sustainability, and occupational health and safety. For students enrolled in 2026-2027 and later, the programme follows a 3+1 model: the first three semesters are spent at school and the fourth semester at a company as Workplace Training. A cumulative GPA of at least 2.00 is required to start Workplace Training.

WORKPLACE TRAINING AND SECTOR COOPERATION

  • Students spend the entire fourth semester (28 ECTS) at a company as Workplace Training. The process is monitored through the Workplace Training Logbook, the workplace evaluation form and supervisor visits.
  • A Workplace Vocational Training and Internship Protocol is signed between the company and the university for each student.
  • The programme has signed Sector Cooperation Letters of Intent with Sonsepet Elektronik Pazarlama Ticaret A.Ş., Karmen Tekstil San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. and Sepette Balık Elektronik Ticaret A.Ş. covering internships and workplace training, expert participation, site visits and joint work.
  • The Advisory Board includes representatives of Trendyol, the Gaziantep Chamber of Industry and e-commerce companies. On the Board's recommendation, the courses E-Commerce Operations Management and Artificial Intelligence Literacy were added to the curriculum, and product safety was included in the E-Commerce Legislation course.
  • Students attend sector talks, career fairs and technical visits.

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

The level of achievement of the Programme Educational Objectives is monitored with the following key performance indicators. Targets apply to the years following the 2025-2026 reference measurement.

  • APG-1.1 — Graduate Employment Rate: Share of graduates employed in any job within the first 12 months after graduation. Target: ≥ 50%
  • APG-1.2 — Field-Related Employment Rate: Share of employed graduates working in e-commerce, digital marketing, brand communication, retail, foreign trade or related sectors. Target: ≥ 40%
  • APG-2.1 — Employer Satisfaction (Professional Competence): Satisfaction of employers with graduates' competence in using current tools, software and processes in e-commerce operations, digital marketing and brand communication. Target: ≥ 3.5 / 5
  • APG-3.1 — Employer Satisfaction (Ethics and Responsibility): Satisfaction of employers with graduates' conduct in line with professional ethics, consumer rights, personal data protection and IT legislation. Target: ≥ 4.0 / 5
  • APG-4.1 — Bachelor's Completion Rate via DGS: Share of graduates placed in a bachelor's programme through the Vertical Transfer Examination (DGS) and continuing their studies. Target: ≥ 25%
  • APG-4.2 — Certificate / Course Participation Rate: Share of graduates attending a professional certificate, course or training programme within the first 12 months after graduation. Target: ≥ 30%

OCCUPATIONAL PROFILES OF GRADUATES

Graduates can work in e-commerce platforms and marketplace companies, digital marketing agencies, retail and chain stores, foreign trade and logistics companies, brand communication agencies, social media and content production companies, and customer service and sales departments. Graduates who obtain a valid score in the KPSS examination may also be employed in the public sector.

ACCESS TO FURTHER STUDIES

Graduates may apply to bachelor's programmes in related fields provided that they obtain a sufficient score in the Vertical Transfer Examination (DGS) conducted by ÖSYM.

GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS

To complete the programme, students must pass all courses, achieve a cumulative GPA of at least 2.00 out of 4.00, and have no FF or FD grades.

MODE OF STUDY

Full-time
