This curriculum applies to students enrolled in the programme in the 2026-2027 academic year and later. The programme totals 124 ECTS; the fourth semester is completed as Workplace Training (28 ECTS).

Course objectives, learning outcomes, weekly topics, assessment methods and ECTS workloads are available in the course information packages in the Gaziantep University Bologna Information System: E-Commerce and Marketing Programme Course Information Packages.
